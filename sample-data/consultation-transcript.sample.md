# Sample Patient Consult transcript (synthetic)

> Illustrative only. Shows the shape of a consultation: instant rule-based severity, a medicine screen, then a doctor referral.

**Patient:** I have a fever of 102 F and a headache for 2 days, 5/10.
**Vitalis AI:** Noted: fever of 102°F (38.9°C), for 2 days, severity 5/10, a headache. Is your neck stiff, or does it hurt to bend your head forward towards your chest?
*(why: with a fever and headache I need to rule out an infection around the brain.)*
**Patient:** No.
**Vitalis AI:** Noted: no stiff neck. Do you have body aches or joint pains?
**Patient:** Yes, a little.
**Vitalis AI:** How old are you, and do you have any existing conditions (diabetes, heart or lung disease, pregnancy)?
**Patient:** I am 39 and I do not have any existing conditions.
**Vitalis AI:** Before I suggest any medicine: are you allergic to any medicines, and do you take any regular medicines?
**Patient:** I am not allergic to any medicine.

**Assessment:** See a doctor soon (within 2-3 days). Suggested specialist: General Physician.
**Over-the-counter options (not a prescription):**
- Paracetamol: 500 mg to 1 g every 4 to 6 hours only if needed; no more than 4 g in 24 hours.
- Ibuprofen: 200 mg to 400 mg every 6 to 8 hours, always with or after food; no more than 1,200 mg in 24 hours without a doctor's advice; stop after 5 days.

**Referral:** Please book a visit with a doctor within the next 2-3 days: these medicines only ease symptoms, they do not treat the cause.
